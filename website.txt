Meus Cortes

A personal desktop tool for Windows that turns my live streams into short vertical clips and publishes or schedules them to my own TikTok account.

What it does
- Finds the best moments of a stream and creates 9:16 clips with captions.
- Lets me review and edit each clip, caption and hashtags.
- Publishes or schedules the clips to my TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API, after I log in with TikTok and choose the privacy and interaction settings of every post.

How it works
The app runs entirely on my computer. Videos and settings stay on my computer and are sent only to TikTok when I publish them. TikTok access is encrypted locally and can be removed at any time.
